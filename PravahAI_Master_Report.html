1 Purpose & System Vision

🧠 Multi-Source Fusion
Dual XGBoost inference + TreeSHAP explainability engine + OSM Dijkstra safe evacuation route & shelter allocator.

2 Technical Honesty & Jury Defense Strategy

⚠️ Mandatory Evaluation & Hackathon Defense Rule:
Low-cost physical analog probes (e.g. 2 cm raindrop sensors) measure surface conductivity and electrical resistance, not calibrated physical millimetres in free atmosphere. In PravahAI, we explicitly label these measurements as "Ground IoT Observation (Simulated Rainfall Equivalent Index)".
Anticipated Jury Question Defensible Technical Answer
"Aapka 2 cm ka rain sensor 200 mm real rainfall kaise measure kar raha hai?" Physical sensor ground-level wetness aur electrical resistance measure karta hai. Humara Calibration Engine use normalized index ($0-100\%$) me convert karta hai aur trained catchment hydrology curve ke hisaab se catchment-equivalent precipitation ($R_{3d} \le 320\text{ mm}$) estimate karta hai. UI par ise clearly 'Simulated Equivalent Index' label kiya gaya hai.
"Agar Satellite Weather aur Local Sensor me difference ho toh model kya karega?" Humne 'Compare Results (Discrepancy Engine)' diya hai — agar satellite dry dikhata hai lekin ground sensor sudden channel surge detect karta hai, toh Agentic AI instantly Localized Flash Flood Warning trigger karta hai without waiting for satellite orbital pass.

4 Mathematical Physics & Calibration Curves

The Python Master Backend converts raw 12-bit ADC values ($0-4095$) into domain hydrological features:

A. Rainfall Scaling (Dry ≈ 4095, Saturated Wet ≈ 400 ADC):
Wetness_Ratio = max(0.0, min(1.0, (4095 - Raw_Rain) / 3600))
Rain_Index_% = Wetness_Ratio × 100%
Scaled_R3d = Wetness_Ratio × 320.0 mm
Scaled_R1h = Wetness_Ratio × 35.0 mm/h
B. River Catchment Depth & Submersion Relative to Danger Benchmark:
Submerged_Ratio = max(0.0, min(1.0, (Raw_Water - 300) / 3200))
River_Gauge_Level (H) = 20.00m + (Submerged_Ratio - 0.40) × 1.60m
Difference_to_Danger = H - 20.00m
River_Discharge (Q) = 220 + (Submerged_Ratio × 1400) m³/s
C. Soil Moisture Saturation Index:
Soil_Wetness_Ratio = max(0.0, min(1.0, (4095 - Raw_Soil) / 3200))
Soil_Saturation_% = min(98.0%, 35.0% + (Soil_Wetness_Ratio × 62.0%))
